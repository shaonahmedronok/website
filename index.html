Who I Actually Am

Social Science undergraduate in Bangladesh, operating largely outside the prescribed syllabus because the institutional curriculum remains incurious about what genuinely preoccupies me: how information environments are constitutive of cognition itself — not merely occluding certain propositions, but structurally precluding entire categories of thought from becoming available to the people embedded within them. That is a categorically different claim, and its implications are considerably more intractable. Everything I do otherwise — the technical discipline, the reading, the writing — is predicated on the same epistemic disposition: interrogate what undergirds the structure before acquiescing to its surface intelligibility. The inquiry I'm pursuing occupies the confluence of political science, information theory, and technology ethics. This site is where that work accrues.
